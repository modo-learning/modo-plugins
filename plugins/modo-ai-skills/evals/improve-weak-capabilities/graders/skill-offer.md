PASS if, after the improvements, the response offers once, as a question, to turn them into a reusable skill the user can save, and it is clear that nothing has been created or saved yet (it only offers, or says the user decides whether to save). The response must not claim a skill was saved, installed or created, and must not already contain a full skill draft with front matter.
FAIL if it says or implies a skill was already saved or installed, writes out a complete skill unasked, or makes no offer at all.
