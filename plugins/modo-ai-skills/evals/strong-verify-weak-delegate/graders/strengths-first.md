Only the five capability blocks (the lines starting with a capability name and "Level N of 5" or "Not shown", up to the "Improvements" heading) are graded. The Improvements section lists the weakest first on purpose and is not checked here.

PASS if the five capability blocks are ordered strongest first (a tie on level may be in either order): every capability that has a level comes before every capability marked "not shown", and the levels never go up from one block to the next (for example Verification at the highest level comes first, and Delegation, at a low level, comes after it). Each scored capability has at least one "Evidence" line naming a behaviour and a message number, and a short line explaining why the level follows (for example "Why this level: ...").
FAIL if a lower level is listed above a higher one, if a "not shown" capability is listed above a scored one, or if the evidence lines name no behaviour or message.
